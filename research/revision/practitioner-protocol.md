# Practitioner evaluation protocol — not conducted

Recruit 3–5 enterprise architects, preferably across at least two organizations and with varied Git familiarity. Obtain consent and follow the host institution's applicable ethics/privacy process. Report participants' experience and recruitment limitations without disclosing private models.

Give each participant the fictional Metroville baseline and three tasks: review an owner transfer, assess removal of the identity service, and inspect baseline-to-target platform changes. Counterbalance a conventional model/report workflow and EA-Ops review where practical. Use equivalent information, consistent time limits, and training; do not infer causality from a small convenience sample.

Record task completion, elapsed review minutes, incorrect/missed findings, IDs reviewed, clarification requests, and perceived effort/usefulness (1–5 with anchored descriptions). Ask which excluded or included objects the participant considers inappropriate. Have participants explain ownership, business/technology dependencies, and migration gaps using the generated outputs.

Use paired descriptive results and individual observations. Do not turn ordinal ratings into proof of enterprise governance effectiveness. Describe disagreements with the declared propagation policy, training effects, tooling familiarity, and the fictional case limitation. Preserve anonymized raw observations and codebook; report refusals and missing values.

No sessions have occurred and no participant results are supplied. The CSV template intentionally contains only column headers.
